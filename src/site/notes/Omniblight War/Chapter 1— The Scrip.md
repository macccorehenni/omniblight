The _Scrip_ looked beautiful, or at the very least, it looked like what Stray would call home. The demigoddess turned mythical creature smiled as she signed the paper to buy the ship. She reasoned it was the best ship offered. The other two weren't big enough, weren't powerful enough for the Omnibalance. Her wings, an alloy of tough dragon hide and brilliant red rubies, folded in with a *shhk*.

"It's all yours. Don't forget to register it." The ship seller smiled, looking over his shoulder at the brilliant red of Stray's wings, tossing her the codecard to board the ship. 

She unlocked the ship, its Soul-core thrumming against the metallic vibrations of her wings. _Yes_, she said, _this is perfect._ She touched the captain's console, and a surge of timespatial energy flowed through her. She pressed a button on the console and the ship warmed up to its idle state. She pulled a lever, and the Soul-core pulsed, as if saying _Thank you_ to its new captain. Stray knew that she'd have to make some slight modifications for timewarping, but as the core got used to her presence it would adapt to her particular resonance.

"_Scrip_–captain to Sol-mid–guild… request takeoff." She liked the feel of the engine as it warmed up, it anticipating what must have been its first trip in quite a while. 

"All right, you should take us to the planet of Pearl," Stray said. "We've got some baddies to kick the butts off of."
