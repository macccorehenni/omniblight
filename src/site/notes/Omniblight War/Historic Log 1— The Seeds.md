_The events in this story are from a thousand years before the_  Scrip _gained its captain. It tells the origin of the Omnibalance— the hero of the story of this war that Stray finds herself in, a war of morals and self, and defines what it means to be human, or divine. It tells the story of how the Second Black Star War  shaped the corruption among the divine that led to the exile of  Stray's found kind, and how it shaped who she was._
It is the year 500 post-Surrender, and another war has left the Galactic Realms in tatters. Whilst the First Black War which lent the shift in calendar its name had been more about control, the Second War had been more about freedom. Amolion, the Guild-Leader, had made a decision to essentially lead the world. The saviors who had won gained their own ships and formed an Armada named after its flagship, the _Armada Vine_. The bounty hunters were the best in the Galaxy, and _that_… that was the problem that led to the War of the Omniblight. 
Secretly, the Black Stars, a group who used humans to further their disdain of Light and her armada, and to further their goals to kill them, secretly and quietly built a fortress on a planet far, far away from Arrisia. They formed the first prototype of what would later be called the Blight, the biomechs. These creatures were injected with a serum that gave controllers complete control over their thoughts and actions. But the Arid Vine Armada was able to reverse engineer it and create their own species, hidden from the public for a long while— the _Omnibalance._ 

It started with them rehabilitating the Titans— the strongest of the biomechs. They became part of the Armada's family. Then, Amalion decided to take it a step farther. With the help of the engineers of the Armada, they developed a sort of proto–Omnibalance. It started with a single brave Armadan, a selkie named Inari who had lost a fin. Amalion and his team of bioengineers grafted a unique alloy of selkie skin and ruby to heal her fin. Not only did it work— but it seemed to corrupt her. Mind you, not in the same way that the biomechs' weaves corrupted them, but in a unique way. Inari started seeing visions— being able to predict the future. She also, inexplicably at first, was able to manipulate those visions. She had become the foundation of what would later become the Omnibalance.

The Black Stars didn't sit idle either. The thought crossed their minds— _what if  we took the biomech weave— and made it into an infectious disease_? Thus the engineers did such, creating the proto-Blight. This is where the story of our protagonist begins. She was born a butterfly angel demigoddess, her mother Asteria her divine side, and an unknown human her mortal side. Suddenly, one day, soon after learning of her father, she suddenly found shunted onto the _Talpari_— the Vine Armada's self–proclaimed anomaly hunting ship. She became one of its gifted members as they took the month-long trip to farspace and the home of Astrellia— Stray's full name. 
They found a net of black crystals poisoning all the lesser divine who had no control over their voices any more— the first of the Blighted. They answered to no one, lest they were punished by the Blightstones, but they had one mission— destroy the Armada…
